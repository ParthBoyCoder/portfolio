Who Am I?

Hey! I'm Parthib Banerjee — a 15-year-old student from Kolkata, India, currently in Class X.

I've been self-teaching programming since Class VI, and somewhere along the way fell completely in love with robotics and AI. I went from blinking LEDs on an Arduino to competing at state-level science exhibitions across India and placing first at Apeejay Terra Nova RoboMaze Runner.

I run a YouTube channel documenting my builds, founded and taught a robotics club at school from Class IX, and build open-source projects that are actually useful. I also do real tech work helping at my father's company since Class VII — AI docs, software debugging, and hardware fixes.

This portfolio is a work in progress, just like me. Last updated: April 2026.
